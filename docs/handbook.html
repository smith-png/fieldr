Overview
System Purpose

What Fieldr Is & Auction Day Flow

Fieldr is a multi-sport player auction platform that coordinates player registration, team purse management, live hall projection, and public rosters from a single database.

Core Concepts

Every student is stored once in the universal Athletes registry with a permanent three-digit code such as #001. For each tournament season, athletes are registered into one or more sports as season players with a base point value and playing role.

During the live auction, the administrator operates the Auction Room while the hall screen displays the Projector view. Every bid, sale, unsold mark, and purse change updates connected screens in real time.

Auction Day Sequence

1. Pre-check Confirm the active season, team starting purses, and registered player pool in Admin.
2. Open Projector Sign in on the hall computer at /projector and set scene 1 Idle or 2 Teams and Purses.
3. Queue Players In the Auction tab, select a sport and add available players to the queue.
4. Run Bidding Bring a player to stage, record floor bids from team owners, and click Secure Player or Mark Unsold.
5. Verify Ledger Review sales and purse balances in Logs and Teams.
Single Operator Control: All bidding takes place verbally or by paddle in the room. Only the Administrator records bids and sales inside Fieldr.

Access Control
Accounts & Permissions

Roles & Logins

Fieldr separates access into three authenticated roles plus open public browsing. Never share administrator credentials on a projector or public machine.

Administrator Signs in at /admin/login using your login id and your password. Full control over seasons, universal athletes, season players, teams, purses, the Auction Room, the Projector Command Studio, activity logs, and site setup.
Team Owner Signs in at /owner/login using the login ID (for example your login id) and password created by the Admin in the Teams Login window. Owners view their live purse, acquired squad, and purse ledger at /owner.
Projector Display Signs in at /projector/login to open /projector. Dedicated read-only stage view for the auditorium projector or LED wall. An already signed-in Admin can also open /projector directly.
Public Viewer No sign-in required. Can browse the homepage, team directory, sport rosters, athlete profiles, past seasons, rules PDFs, handbook PDFs, and credits.
Team Owners Never Bid in the App: Team Owner accounts are strictly read-only dashboards for tracking budgets and rosters. Owners never place bids from their phones or laptops.

Public Pages
Spectator & Student Experience

The Public Site

Students and spectators can follow rosters, team squads, and official documents from any phone or browser without signing in.

Home (/)

Displays the live announcement ticker, active season indicator, animated counters for athletes, sports, and teams, sport cards, and quick links to rosters and rules.

Teams (/teams)

Lists franchises for the active season with sport filters and search. Each team card displays the team logo or short code, sport badge, team name, Franchise Owner, Roster Size (player count), and a View Squad link. Team purse balances are hidden on public team cards.

Rosters (/rosters & /rosters/:sport)

Shows sport cards with total, sold, and available counts. Inside a sport roster, visitors can filter by All, Available, Sold, or Unsold, filter by role or year, and search by name, roll number, or code.

Player Profiles (/players/:athleteCode)

Each athlete has a shareable profile URL such as /players/001 showing their photo, academic year, roll number, active season sport registrations, and historical appearances across seasons.

Seasons (/seasons)

Archive of current and past seasons. Visitors can open any season and inspect its franchises and final squads.

Rules (/rules) & Handbook (/handbook)

Provides in-page PDF previews and direct download links for official tournament rules and handbooks, plus /credits for the organizing and engineering team.

Admin Guide
Sidebar Tabs: Dashboard & Athletes

Dashboard & Universal Athletes

The Admin sidebar at /admin contains a season switcher at the top and eight navigation tabs: Dashboard, Athletes, Teams, Auction, Logs, Seasons, Projector, and Setup.

Dashboard Tab

Provides an immediate overview of the active season with a Switch Projector Scene button in the header and four metric cards:

  • Registered Teams: Active franchises and sport count.
  • Player Pool: Total season players with sold, available, and unsold breakdown.
  • Total Active Purse: Combined remaining points across all teams.
  • Purse Transactions: Count of ledger entries.

Below the metrics, Recent Purse Activity lists the five latest transactions with a Manage purses link.

Athletes Tab (Top Section)

Manages the permanent student database across all seasons. The table displays code (#001), name, academic year, roll number, and active season status (no thumbnail column in the table).

  • Click Add Athlete or Edit to set name, academic year, roll number, and upload a player photo.
  • Filter by All, In Active Season, or Not in Active Season, or by academic year.
  • Click Register on any row to enroll that athlete into a sport for the active season.

Sidebar Tab: Athletes (Lower Section)

Registered Season Players

Directly below the universal Athletes table on the same Athletes tab is the Registered Players section for the active season.

Bulk & Single Registration

Click Register Athletes in Bulk to expand the bulk registration panel:

  • Choose a target sport, default playing role, and default base points.
  • Search or filter universal athletes by academic year and select multiple students.
  • Optionally override playing role or base points per student, then click Register Selected.

For a single student, click Register Athlete to search the universal list and assign their sport, role, and base points.

Managing Season Entries & Export

Each season registration row shows the player photo thumbnail, name, code, sport, role, academic year, base points, status, and assigned team.

  • Click the team assignment icon (Assign Team) to manually assign a player to a matching-sport team at a specified price (or 0 PTS) or unassign them.
  • Click Export CSV to download a 4-column file (Athlete Code,Name,Academic Year,Roll Number) for offline sheets.
Single Realtime Channel: While the merged Athletes tab is open, universal athletes and season players share a single admin_athletes_realtime subscription so updates stay synchronized without duplicate connections.

Sidebar Tab: Teams

Teams, Owner Logins & Purses

The Teams tab combines franchise management at the top with the Purses & Financial Ledger section directly below the team cards.

Sports, Teams & Owner Accounts

  • Sports & Roles: Expand Sports & Roles to add a sport or manage its allowed playing roles.
  • New Team & Import CSV: Create franchises individually with New Team (name, short code, sport, owner name, starting purse, logo) or batch-create via Import CSV.
  • Teams Login: Click Teams Login to create a Team Owner login ID and password, assign an owner to a team, or reset their password.

Purses & Financial Ledger

Below the team cards, the purses section displays summary balances and two primary controls:

  • Update Purse: Select a team, choose Add Points, Deduct Points, or Set Exact Purse, enter the amount and reason, and click Execute Transaction.
  • Reset All Purses: Sets both the starting purse and current purse of every team in the active season to a new amount.
  • Transaction History: Filterable ledger of every manual adjustment and auction deduction.
Reset All Purses Warning: Running Reset All Purses overwrites the current purse balance of every team in the active season. Only use it before the auction begins.

Sidebar Tabs: Seasons, Logs & Setup

Seasons, Activity Logs & Site Setup

Manage tournament editions, audit every sale or purse adjustment, and configure public site content from three dedicated tabs.

Seasons Tab

Click New Season to create a tournament edition, or click Make Active on an existing card to switch the entire platform to that season. Deleting an inactive season previews how many teams, players, and transactions will be removed while preserving all universal athletes.

Logs Tab (/admin/logs)

Chronological audit feed of the active season. Filter events by All, Sales, Unsold, Purse, or Reversed, filter by franchise, or search by player, team, or reason.

Setup Tab

  • Homepage Ticker: Add, edit, reorder, or toggle announcement items shown on the public homepage (at least one active item is required).
  • Rules PDFs: Upload or delete PDF rulebooks for the public /rules page.
  • Credits Page: Manage people, roles, links, and photos displayed on /credits.
  • Danger Zone: Season reset utilities requiring confirmation by typing RESET.

Sidebar Tab: Projector

Projector Command Studio

The Projector tab controls what the auditorium display at /projector shows between live bidding rounds.

Scene 1: Idle Displays the standby tournament screen with the active season title, live status indicator, and a custom headline message.
Scene 2: Teams and Purses Highlights a single selected franchise or enables Auto Rotate All Teams to cycle through every franchise card with its remaining purse and squad count.
Scene 3: Player Spotlight Showcases one chosen player or enables Auto Rotate Players filtered by All Players, Available, Sold, or Unsold, plus an optional sport filter.
Intervals & Motion Auto-rotation intervals accept any value from 3s to 120s (default 8s), with quick preset chips for 5s, 8s, 10s, and 15s. Choose Fade, Slide, or Zoom transitions, then click Send to Projector.
Live Auction Stage Guard: Whenever a player is actively on stage in the Auction Room (auction, sold, or unsold mode), the Projector tab displays a warning banner so you do not accidentally interrupt a live sale.

Live Auction
Sidebar Tab: Auction (Part 1)

Auction Room: Queue & Bidding

The Auction tab is the live command desk used during bidding. Start by selecting a sport pill at the top of the workspace.

1. Building the Queue & Stage

  • Bulk Add by Codes: Paste three-digit athlete codes (001 to 999) and click Add to Queue.
  • Player List & Queue Order: Add individual available players, move queued rows up or down, or click Clear Queue.
  • Bringing to Stage: Click Bring to Stage on any player, Next Available to call the top queued player, or Random Available. The hall projector immediately switches to the live bidding view.

2. Recording Bids

Only teams registered in the same sport as the stage player can bid. Teams with insufficient purse or a full squad are disabled automatically.

  • Click a team card to select the bidding franchise.
  • Click Base for the opening bid, click a quick increment button (for example +5, +10, +25), or type an exact amount and click Apply.
  • If a floor bid was entered by mistake, click Clear Current Bid to void the active bids for the player on stage and reset the leading team.

Live Auction
Sidebar Tab: Auction (Part 2)

Securing Sales, Unsold & Undo

Closing a bidding round updates the player status, team purse, ledger, and hall projector in a single database transaction.

Secure Player Requires a leading team on stage. Clicking Secure Player marks the player sold, assigns them to the winning team, deducts the sale price from the team's current_purse, records a purse deduction and sale row, and triggers the celebration sequence on the projector.
Mark Unsold Clicking Mark Unsold sets the stage player's status to unsold, clears any active bid on stage, and switches the projector to the unsold state. Unsold players can be brought back to stage later if re-auctioned.
Undo last sale Opens a confirmation dialog for the most recent unreversed sale in the active season. Confirming calls auction_undo_last_sale, which marks that sale record with a reversed_at timestamp, sets the player's status back to available (clearing their team and purchase price), refunds the full sale price back to the team's current_purse with a reversal transaction, and returns the projector to idle if that sold player is still on screen.
Settings & Back to Idle Click Back to Idle to clear the stage between rounds. Click Settings to configure bid increments, maximum squad size, celebration duration (2s to 15s), and price count-up speed.

Franchise View
Owner Experience

Team Owner Panel

When a Team Owner signs in at /owner/login, Fieldr opens their franchise dashboard at /owner (or their assigned team page if linked to a single franchise).

What a Team Owner Can See

  • Financial Summary: Private cards showing Remaining Purse, Total Spent, Players Bought, and Starting Purse.
  • Live Auction Feed: A real-time banner showing which player is currently on stage and the current leading bid.
  • Acquired Players: Full table of players bought by the franchise, including role, academic year, and purchase price.
  • Purse History: Chronological ledger of every auction deduction and admin adjustment for their team.

What a Team Owner Cannot Do

  • No Bidding Controls: Owners cannot place, raise, or clear bids from their account.
  • No Roster Edits: Owners cannot add, remove, or trade players.
  • No Other Team Ledgers: Owners can only view private financial history for the franchise assigned to their login.
  • No Stage Control: Owners cannot change projector scenes or auction settings.

Tester Checklist & Reporting

Run through this checklist before auction day to verify that accounts, projector scenes, and realtime updates work as expected on venue WiFi.

Pre-Auction Tester Checklist

  • Sign in at /admin/login and confirm the intended season is marked active in Seasons.
  • Create or edit a test athlete in Athletes, upload a photo, and register them into a sport below.
  • Verify team cards and starting purses in Teams, and test an owner login at /owner/login.
  • Open /projector on a second screen; switch between scenes 1 Idle, 2 Teams and Purses, and 3 Player Spotlight.
  • In Auction, bring the test player to stage, apply a bid, click Secure Player, and test Undo last sale.
  • Check /teams and /rosters in an incognito window to confirm public views load cleanly.

Why is a team button disabled during bidding?

Either the team belongs to a different sport, its remaining purse is lower than the next bid amount, or its squad has reached the configured maximum squad size.

What if a bid is entered for the wrong team?

Before securing the sale, click Clear Current Bid in the Auction Room. If the sale was already secured, click Undo last sale to reverse the sale, restore the player to available, and refund the team's purse.

Can one athlete play in multiple sports?

Yes. A universal athlete keeps one permanent code (#001) and can be registered into multiple sports within the same season.

System Architecture & Data Model

Fieldr is a client-rendered single-page application built with React, Vite, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS v4 (built with Google AI Studio), hosted on Vercel with automatic deployments from a private GitHub repository, and backed by Supabase.

Core Data Model

athletes Universal student records (athlete_code, name, year, roll no, photos).
seasons & sports Tournament editions (is_active) and sports with role arrays.
players Per-season registrations linking an athlete, season, sport, role, base points, status, and team.
teams Season franchises with starting_purse and current_purse.
purse_transactions Immutable ledger of add, deduct, set, and initial events.
projector_state Single active row per season driving the hall display mode and stage player.
auction_queue / bids / sales Per-sport queue order, bid log (voided), and sale records (reversed_at).
auction_settings Per-season increments, max squad size, and celebration timings.

Security & Transactional Data Flow

Access is enforced at both the router layer and the Postgres Row Level Security (RLS) layer, with all multi-table auction and purse mutations executed inside security definer RPCs.

Auth, RLS & Public Isolation

Supabase Auth sessions map to a profiles row with role admin, owner, or projector, checked by RequireAdmin, RequireOwner, and RequireProjectorAuth. Team Owner accounts are provisioned and updated through the manage-owner Edge Function.

Public pages read only from restricted views (public_players, public_teams, public_squads), public tables (seasons, sports, carousel_items, rules_documents, credits_people), and handbook_list. Signed-in owners load private team purse data via owner_team_overview.

Admin-Checked RPCs

All stage, queue, and purse writes execute through transactional Postgres RPCs:

auction_bring_to_stage, auction_place_bid, auction_clear_bid, auction_secure, auction_mark_unsold, auction_undo_last_sale, queue_add_player, queue_add_by_codes, queue_remove, queue_clear, queue_move, execute_purse_transaction, reset_all_purses, assign_player_to_team, unassign_player_from_team, rename_sport_role, rename_season, delete_season, admin_activity_log

Critical Security Rule: Never commit service-role keys, database passwords, or access secrets to client code or documentation.

Realtime, Performance & Operations

Realtime subscriptions are restricted strictly to Admin, Auction Room, and Projector screens so public visitors on college WiFi never consume WebSocket connections.

Realtime Channels

  • projector_state_${seasonId}, players_${seasonId}, and teams_${seasonId}: opened by AdminPage and ProjectorPage.
  • auction_settings_${seasonId}: opened by ProjectorPage.
  • admin_auction_live_${seasonId}: opened by AdminAuction (watches projector_state, players, teams, auction_bids, auction_sales, auction_settings, and auction_queue).
  • admin_athletes_realtime: single channel opened by AdminAthletes on the merged Athletes tab.

Projector State Machine

Modes are idle, teams, player_spotlight, auction, sold, and unsold, with spotlight filters all, available, sold, and unsold and rotation intervals clamped to 3s–120s (default 8s).

Pagination, Thumbnails & WiFi Scale

Admin Athletes paginates at 25 rows per page and public Rosters at 24 cards per page. Uploaded player photos (max 5 MB) generate a 400px JPEG thumbnail (0.82 quality) in fieldr-assets so lists load lightweight thumbnails while the projector pre-decodes full images. Public pages use in-memory caching for reliable performance on shared college WiFi.

Deployment, Rollback & Plan Limits

Pushes to the main branch auto-deploy on Vercel. To roll back instantly during an event, open the Vercel deployments dashboard and click Promote to Production on the last known-good build. Monitor Supabase concurrent Realtime connections, Storage bandwidth, and database egress prior to auction day.
